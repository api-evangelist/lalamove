The “last-mile” delivery. It’s a phrase that has become much more commonplace during the past few years, not surprising with eye-catching stories about drone delivery and even autonomous robots delivering pizza to dorm rooms. But the issue of getting the last mile right - traditionally getting a product from a logistics hub to the end-consumer’s home or workplace - can be an expensive one.
