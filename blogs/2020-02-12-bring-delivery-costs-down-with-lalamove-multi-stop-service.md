Last mile deliveries can be the most expensive part of the entire product journey along the supply chain. They can be time consuming, reliant upon the delivery recipient’s availability and require a significant amount of manpower from drivers. This all means that if last mile deliveries are not executed smartly then the costs can quickly add up and risk taking a big chunk out of your profits.
